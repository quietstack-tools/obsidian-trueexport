# TrueExport — Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 — 17 August 2026**

This policy explains what personal information TrueExport and I, Kesavan
Paripurapavan (ABN 94 867 243 153), collect, why, and what you can do about it.

## The short version

TrueExport runs entirely on your own computer. It has no analytics, no
telemetry, no crash reporting and no update checking. Your note content never
leaves your device. The only information I hold about you is what is needed to
deliver and support a Pro licence key.

## What I collect

If you buy TrueExport Pro, my payment processor, **Polar Software Inc.**
("Polar"), acts as merchant of record for the transaction and passes me:

- your email address;
- your order number; and
- your licence key.

I do not collect anything else. In particular, I do not receive your card
details, your note content, or any information about how you use TrueExport.

Polar separately collects and processes payment information (such as card
details and billing address) as merchant of record for the transaction. That
processing is governed by Polar's own privacy policy, not this one — see
[Polar's Privacy Policy](https://polar.sh/legal/privacy-policy) for details.

## Why I use it

I use your email address, order number and licence key only to:

- deliver your licence key and confirm your purchase;
- provide support if you contact me;
- process a refund or reset activations if you ask; and
- meet my own legal and accounting obligations (e.g. record-keeping).

I do not use this information for marketing, profiling, or any purpose beyond
the above, and I do not sell it or share it with anyone other than Polar (as
my payment processor) and any service provider strictly necessary to run
support email (e.g. my email host).

## Legal basis (for users in the EU/UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, my legal basis for this processing is
performance of a contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) — I need this information to
deliver what you purchased and support it.

## How long I keep it

I keep order and support records for as long as needed to meet my tax and
accounting obligations (currently five years under Australian tax law), after
which I delete them unless you ask me to delete them sooner and I am not
required to retain them.

## Your rights

You can ask me, at any time, by emailing **support@quietstack.tools**, to:

- tell you what information I hold about you;
- correct it if it's wrong; or
- delete it, subject to my legal obligation to retain transaction records for
  accounting purposes.

If you are in the EU or UK, these are your rights under the GDPR / UK GDPR. If
you are in Australia, I extend the same rights to you as a matter of policy.
I will respond within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your information, you can also
complain to the **Office of the Australian Information Commissioner** (if you
are in Australia) or to your local data protection authority (if you are in
the EU or UK). I'd appreciate the chance to sort it out directly first —
email me and I will do my best to resolve it.

## Security

I don't hold enough personal information to make elaborate security
infrastructure meaningful, but I take reasonable steps to protect what I do
hold — including keeping access to my order records limited to me, and using
a reputable, encrypted email provider.

## Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will update the version number and date at the top
and note what changed. Material changes will be flagged in the plugin's
CHANGELOG.
